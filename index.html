About Toolora

Toolora offers free browser-based tools for everyday tasks, including calculations, text counting, QR code creation, and image compression. Our goal is to make common tasks simple and accessible without requiring an account.

How to use Toolora

Get a result in just a few simple steps.

  1. Choose the tool you need from the list above.
  2. Enter the required number, date, text, link, or image.
  3. Press the tool's action button to calculate or create your result.
  4. Review your result and use the copy or download option when available.

Most tools run directly in your browser. The QR Code Generator uses an external service to create QR images, so avoid entering private or confidential information.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using these free tools.

Are Toolora tools free?

Yes. The tools on this page are offered free to use, and you do not need to create an account.

Do I need to install an app?

No installation is required. Open this website in a modern browser on your phone, tablet, or computer.

Is my text uploaded when I use the word counter?

The word counter counts text in your browser. For privacy, do not enter confidential information into third-party services, including the QR generator.

How does the discount calculator work?

Enter the original price and discount percentage. The tool calculates the amount saved and the price after the discount.

Can I rely on every result for official decisions?

These tools are for general use. Please independently verify any result that matters for financial, legal, medical, or official decisions.
